# OCTIVO LABS — "The Fire of Stories" · Art Direction

One continuous descending shot: scattered stars → gathered fire.
The user's brand image (violet flame amphitheatre, learners gathered) is the film's DESTINATION.

## Type
- **Display:** Fraunces (high-contrast expressive serif — the "stories" voice). Big optical size, occasional italic.
- **Body/UI:** Space Grotesk (the "space/systems" voice). Letterspaced caps for labels.

## Logo lockup
Inline SVG: OCTIVO in Fraunces, an orbital ellipse passing through the second O, a 4-point spark at the orbit's apex. LABS in letterspaced Space Grotesk beneath.

## Motion feel
Weightless, gravitational. Frame lerp 0.10. Long envelopes, slow inOut eases. Nothing snaps; everything drifts.

## Chapters (film readout: "DESCENT" altimeter, 428 km → 0)
1. **THE VOID** — deep space, ringed planet, empty center (wordmark lives here)
2. **CONSTELLATIONS** — falling; stars connect into glowing story-shapes
3. **THE VEIL** — through a violet nebula, cold indigo warms to magenta
4. **CRYSTALFIELD** — dark landscape of crystalline spires, purple glow below
5. **THE GATHERING** — the amphitheatre, the violet flame, learners with lit screens; constellation spirals from the fire (= brand image)

## Style bible (append to every generation prompt)
"Stylized flat vector illustration, cinematic wide shot; deep indigo night palette (#070722, #12124A) with violet and magenta glow accents (#7B2FF7, #E04AFB); fine white starfield; soft luminous gradients; clean geometric shapes; subtle atmosphere; no text, no watermark, no logo."

## Camera law
Always descending, slow constant fall. No reversals, no pans against the fall. Uniform 5s clips.
