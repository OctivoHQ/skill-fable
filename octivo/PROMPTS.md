# The Fire of Stories — chapter prompts (Seedance 2.0, 5s each, 16:9, audio OFF)

STYLE (appended to every prompt): Stylized flat vector illustration, cinematic wide
shot; deep indigo night palette (#070722, #12124A) with violet and magenta glow accents
(#7B2FF7, #E04AFB); fine white starfield; soft luminous gradients; clean geometric
shapes; subtle atmosphere; no text, no watermark, no logo.

CAMERA LAW: one continuous slow vertical descent, constant speed, no cuts, no
reversals, no shake. Continuation clips: "Continue the exact same shot from the
reference frame, identical framing, identical colour grade. Do not change the colour
grade."

## C1 — THE VOID (start: keyframe)
Continue the exact same shot from the reference frame. The camera begins a slow,
weightless vertical descent through deep space. Stars drift gently upward past the
frame. The ringed planet in the upper right slides slowly up and out of view. Faint
violet nebula wisps at the bottom edge grow very slightly closer. Constant slow
descent, no cuts. Identical framing, identical colour grade.

## C2 — CONSTELLATIONS (start: C1 last frame)
Continue the exact same shot from the reference frame, identical framing, identical
colour grade. The slow vertical descent continues. As stars drift upward past the
camera, thin luminous violet lines begin connecting star to star, drawing glowing
constellation patterns — an open book shape, an orbit ring, a rising spark — that
glide upward past the frame as the camera falls through them. Soft glow pulses along
the lines. Constant slow descent, no cuts. Do not change the colour grade.

## C3 — THE VEIL (start: C2 last frame)
Continue the exact same shot from the reference frame, identical framing, identical
colour grade. The descent continues into a soft violet nebula rising from below. Silky
layers of indigo and violet cloud part gently around the camera as it falls through
them, glow warming subtly from cold indigo toward magenta at the bottom of frame.
Faint embers of light drift upward. Constant slow descent, no cuts. Do not change the
colour grade.

## C4 — CRYSTALFIELD (start: C3 last frame)
Continue the exact same shot from the reference frame, identical framing, identical
colour grade. The camera descends out of the last thin nebula veils and a dark
landscape resolves below: tall crystalline spires and angular standing stones on a
shadowed plain, edges catching violet and magenta light, a warm magenta glow rising
from a hollow between the crystals at lower center. The glow grows slowly nearer as
the camera keeps falling toward it. Constant slow descent, no cuts. Do not change the
colour grade.

## C5 — THE GATHERING (start: C4 last frame)
Continue the exact same shot from the reference frame, identical framing, identical
colour grade. The slow descent settles toward a circular stone amphitheatre between
the crystals: a wide shallow bowl at its center holds a tall violet-magenta flame,
glowing bright. Five people sit and stand on the encircling stone steps, faces lit by
their tablets and laptops, gathered around the fire. A delicate spiral of glowing
constellation nodes and lines rises from the flame into the sky. The camera eases to a
gentle stop framing the whole gathering, flame at center. No cuts. Do not change the
colour grade.
